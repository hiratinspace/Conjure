I

Point

Move your hand and the cursor follows. Touch each rune to light it.

A small, rested movement covers the whole screen after calibration.

II

Pinch

Point at the candle, then give a quick pinch: snap thumb and index finger together.

Two quick pinches make a double-click; thumb to middle finger is a right-click. If the dot by the cursor turns red, pinch a little faster.

III

Hold still

No pinch needed. Rest the cursor on the crystal until the ring fills.

For hands that can't pinch. Move away and back to click again.

IV

Your own spell

Point at the door and cast the gesture you recorded. Any motion your hand can repeat becomes a click.

Record a spell from the Conjure panel. It is named, saved, and spoken when cast.

V

Scroll

Raise two fingers, then lift or lower your hand to read to the end of the scroll.

Conjure turns a standard webcam into a mouse.

It was built for people who live with tremor, arthritis, partial paralysis, or repetitive strain, and for whom a mouse is hard or painful.

Every other webcam pointer asks your hand to learn its gestures. Conjure learns yours.

Calibration maps the small movement you can make comfortably onto the whole screen.

Smoothing steadies a shaking hand. Precision mode slows the cursor as your hand slows, so small targets stay within reach.

When your hand drops out of view to rest, Conjure pauses, and nothing can click by accident.

Everything runs on this laptop. No video leaves it.

You reached the end of the scroll.

VI

Drag

Pinch the moonstone, carry it to the cauldron, and let go. Or click the stone, then click the cauldron.

VII

The spell is complete

Pointing, clicking, holding, casting, scrolling, and dragging, with a webcam and one hand.
